Controlled comparison with [the tolerance-0.5 check](t05_correctness_2026-09-05.md).
Same three bank-verified raw ingredients, explicit H, all source seeds,
branch cap 100, no sweep, no candidate cap. Only detection/index tolerance
changed to 1.0. Core AAM defaults and search logic were not modified.

## Result

- At 0.5: best one-copy-per-ingredient cover was 53/58 explicit atoms.
- At 1.0: the saved occupations provide **58/58**, using one copy of each.
- Acetylacetone's five carbons map to P0, P1, P15, P16, P17, the intended
  central backbone. Its single bond can now match P1=P15.
- Among maximum-coverage combinations, the smallest overlap is one H (P30),
  supplied by both acetylacetone and the benzothiazole. The viewer shows it grey.
  This is complete geometric support, not exclusive atom ownership or a
  chemically validated atom map. In particular this does not assert that every
  atom follows the literature reaction mechanism.
- The selected relations were checked for element identity, injectivity within
  each R, all 58 target atoms in the union, and support for every carried bond
  under tolerance 1.0. No atomwise mixtures or manually assigned mappings.
- The benzothiazole cap means its matching alternatives are incomplete.

The known-set check consumes the saved production occupation index. It does
not inject a candidate into the recommendation stream. Coverage-equivalent
relations may share a witness for this diagnostic's coverage objective only;
the full production index retains their fragment relations.

## Ranking remains separate

The unrestricted production merge (432177) built its occupation index in
33.805 seconds and saved its decision graph, but had not emitted an assembly
when canceled at 3 min 17 s after the requested known-set check succeeded.
No recommendation rank is claimed. This exposes a separate post-processing
cost; it does not invalidate the recovered known-set cover. All detection
checkpoints and merge intermediates remain saved.
