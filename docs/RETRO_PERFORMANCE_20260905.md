## What changed

- Identity transport does no reconstruction; repeated generators share one
  conjugation calculation per coordinate frame.
- Hierarchy views retain an immutable base plus a correlated target action.
- Search paths cache their typed hierarchy, and source partitions share their
  equivalence analysis across target placements.
- Generators fixing the entire current occupation are skipped exactly.
- Detection archives share fragment and permutation tables. Existing v4 records
  can be repacked without matching; no candidate or generator is discarded.

The example's JSON decreased from 11,237,255 to 1,410,786 bytes. The reduced
file size comes from references, not reduced matching evidence.

## Full-bank attempts

The historical 6.76552 s result covered all **1,919 inventory compounds**, with
28 shards × 48 CPUs. It was not the larger merged Mcule bank.

The unoptimized refactor attempt (`431912`, replacement shard `431940`) was
stopped near the ten-minute watchdog. A first transport/shared-fragment revision
(`431943`) also did not complete any full shard before the watchdog. Later
generator pooling and support/partition caching are verified on the exact
example and tests, **not yet on a completed full-bank scan**. Assembly was not
started on either incomplete scan. Do not report a full-bank speedup.

There is a material policy difference: the historical workflow augmented only
the largest initial families and had a candidate cap and attachment restrictions.
The current workflow augments every initial family, including small ones, and
has neither restriction. The user declined restoring largest-first selection;
the current policy continues to retain every initial family.
