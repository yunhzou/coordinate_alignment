How the comparison was checked

AAM uses one seed order, branch cap 1000 and tolerance 1.0. Native XYZ SLAP is shown both without cuts and with the uncut plus every individual edge deletion in both directions. Every pattern is scored on the original full-H WBO matrices: bond presence > 0.2, order-change magnitude > 0.5. This catalogue holds the benchmark's recorded minimum score fixed.

The analysis scans all saved full AAM terminal witnesses, enumerates SLAP label-family event patterns, and queries the correlated AAM families. “Excluded” requires completed membership checks or completed enumeration. An unfinished enumeration is a lower bound and cannot prove absence. Additional AAM alternatives may exist where enumeration remains incomplete.

The downloadable report includes mappings, compressed-family witnesses, completeness flags, source hashes and independent finite checks. No mapping search or benchmark timing was rerun.
