What is shown, ranking, and saved archives

The detected choices are up to three distinct raw-heavy representatives ranked across the saved random and distance-policy archives. Ranking prioritizes product heavy coverage, total coverage, then fewer explicit-H bond events. These are representative assignments, not enumeration of every mapping inside the compressed families. No reference labels are used to rank them.

Ground-truth colors show conserved regions derived from its mapping, not AAM fragments. Detected colors show actual saved AAM fragments. Across columns, a color is reused only for the same set of source heavy atoms (H-only groups separately). Different decomposition can therefore change colors; r-labels remain stable.

Comparable bond counts below the headings include heavy atoms only because reference H identities are not annotated. Search and the detected ranking include explicit H.
